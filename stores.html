How to read any store in ninety seconds

The directory tells you where to go. This tells you how to move once you are inside, regardless of the chain.

01 · PERIMETER

Walk the walls first

Fill the cart with meat, eggs, fish, dairy and produce before you ever turn into a centre aisle. Most trips can end there.

02 · THE COUNTER

Talk to the butcher

Counter staff will grind fresh, portion, and special-order organ meats and bones — usually cheaper than the pre-packed case.

03 · PRIVATE LABEL

Find the clean sub-brand

Almost every chain has one honest organic/clean line — Simple Truth, SO Organic, Macro, Bio. Learn its name and lean on it.

04 · UNIT PRICE

Read the small number

The price-per-pound or per-kilo on the shelf tag, not the sticker price, is how you actually compare value across brands.

05 · FROZEN

Frozen is not lesser

Frozen wild fish and frozen produce are picked at peak and often cleaner and cheaper than tired "fresh" equivalents.

06 · THE LIST

Turn the package over

Ignore the front entirely. If the ingredient list is short and legible, it stays. If not, it goes back on the shelf.
