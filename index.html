Why this exists

The store is designed to confuse you. This is the map.

Most of what fills a modern supermarket was engineered to be bought, not to nourish you — refined starch, industrial seed oil and added sugar, dressed up in packaging that sells the very health it destroyed. "Natural," "made with whole grain," "a good source of fibre": the loudest health claims sit on the products that need them most. Meanwhile the food that has actually kept humans alive for millennia sits quietly around the edges of the store, usually cheaper, with no marketing budget at all.

You already sense this. What you don't have is the time to decode every label, cross-check every brand, and figure out what's worth buying at the specific store near you. That is the entire job this guide does for you — once, thoroughly, so you never have to think about it in the aisle again.

A tomato has no marketing budget. Neither does a lamb chop, a dozen pastured eggs, or a tin of sardines. This is the guide to the quiet corner of the store where your health has always been.
