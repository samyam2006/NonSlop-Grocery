The Real-Food Cheat Sheet
Shop the edges. Trust the ingredient list. Eat the animal, not the imitation.

✓ Buy — build the cart from these

Meat & eggs

  • Grass-fed / grass-finished beef & lamb
  • Beef liver, heart, oxtail, marrow bones
  • Pasture-raised eggs
  • Pastured / organic chicken (whole is cheapest)
  • Bacon & sausage with no added sugar or fillers

Seafood

  • Wild salmon (sockeye), sardines, mackerel
  • Tinned fish in olive oil or water
  • Shellfish — oysters, mussels, shrimp

Dairy & fats

  • Butter & ghee (grass-fed if you can)
  • Full-fat plain yogurt & kefir
  • Raw-milk / aged cheeses
  • Extra-virgin olive oil, coconut oil, tallow

Produce

  • Leafy greens, crucifers, berries, avocados
  • Onions, garlic, mushrooms, squash
  • Fresh herbs, lemons, ginger

Pantry

  • Nuts & seeds (raw, unsalted)
  • Olives, honey (raw), real maple
  • Sea salt, single-ingredient spices
  • Fermented: sauerkraut, kimchi, kefir

✕ Skip — put it back

Industrial seed oils

Soybean · canola · corn · "vegetable" · sunflower · safflower · cottonseed · grapeseed · rice bran. Hidden in nearly all packaged & fried food.

Sugar, under every alias

Corn syrup · HFCS · cane juice · dextrose · maltodextrin · "fruit concentrate" · agave · rice syrup. If it's in the first 3 ingredients, put it back.

Fake sugars

Sucralose · aspartame · acesulfame-K. Erythritol in moderation at most.

Ultra-processed flags

  • Ingredient list longer than your hand
  • Words you can't pronounce / say "modified"
  • Artificial colors (Red 40, Yellow 5/6)
  • "Natural flavors," emulsifiers, gums stacked up
  • Enriched / bleached wheat flour

Deprioritize

Non-organic oats & corn (glyphosate / GMO), sugary "health" bars, flavored yogurts, deli meats with additives.

⦿ Read the label in 90 seconds

  • "Grass-fed" ≠ "grass-finished." Most grass-fed beef is still grain-finished. Look for 100% grass-fed/finished.
  • "Natural" means nothing. It's marketing, not a standard. "Organic" and "Certified" do mean something.
  • "No hormones" on chicken/pork is meaningless (already banned). It's only meaningful on beef.
  • Front vs back. The front is an ad; the back (ingredients) is the law. When they disagree, believe the back.
  • PLU codes. 5-digit starting 9 = organic. (The "8 = GMO" rule was never actually used — ignore it.)
  • Short is better. Real food is usually 1–5 recognizable ingredients.

↔ Once you're inside

  1. Shop the perimeter — meat, fish, eggs, dairy, produce.
  2. Only dip into center aisles for known staples (oil, tinned fish, nuts, spices).
  3. Buy the cheap real thing before the expensive fake thing.
  4. Full-fat, plain, single-ingredient — every time.
